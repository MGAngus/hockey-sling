Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short

  • I don't collect any personal data myself. There are no accounts and no analytics.
  • Your game progress stays on your iPhone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time.

Who is responsible

Hockey Sling is made by Riku Kruuse, an independent developer based in Finland. For any privacy question or request, email rk62games@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your progress (unlocked levels, stars and best scores), your sound and vibration settings, which tips and chapter introductions you have already seen, and a count of the levels you have completed since the last ad, so that ads are spaced out. This data stays on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Hockey Sling is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google: an optional video ad that gives you an extra puck, and an ad after every fifth level you complete. When an ad is requested, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking, and identifiers that are limited to this app or developer
  • advertising data, such as which ads you have seen
  • interactions, such as ad taps and app launches
  • crash logs and performance data

Google uses this data to show ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud and improve its services. If you allow it, ads can be personalized based on your activity in other apps and websites. I only receive aggregated reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown, and nothing that identifies you.

Read more in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message when you first open it. Your choice is stored on your device. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time: tap the ⓘ button on the title screen and then Privacy settings.
  • Tracking. iOS asks whether Hockey Sling may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, the game works the same and still shows ads, but the advertising identifier isn't available to Google.
  • Optional ads. Watching an ad for an extra puck is always your choice.
  • Reporting ads. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, tap the ⓘ button on the title screen and then Report an ad, or email me.

Legal basis

Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and the UK, personalized ads and storing or reading information on your device for ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Other ad processing that Google carries out where the law allows it, such as showing non-personalized ads and preventing fraud, is based on the legitimate interest in offering a free, ad-supported game.

Third parties

The only third party that receives data through the app is Google, as described above. I only use third-party services that provide the same or equal protection of user data as described in this policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Google keeps advertising data for the periods described in its privacy policy. To revoke your consent, use Privacy settings in the game or the Tracking setting in iOS, as described above. Because I don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing for me to delete, but you can email me with any request and I'll help you. For data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Hockey Sling is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Finland, that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The pages themselves use no cookies, analytics or other trackers.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
